**FACULTY OF COMPUTING & INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY**

**Undergraduate Project Documentation Guide**

**Purpose:** This brief guide is meant to provide some information about
writing a project proposal and full documentation at Faculty of
Computing & Information Technology, Multimedia University of Kenya. It
gives the general format, and highlights the purpose of each section,
the required content and gives some pointers.

**General guidelines**

-   Font: Times New Romans, Size 12 with 1.5 spacing

-   Paragraph spacing: 6pts After

-   Margins: Top, Bottom, Right -- 1 inch; Left -- 1.5 inches

-   Alignment: Justified

-   Page Size: A4

-   Page numbering: Bottom centered, with X of Y format. Preliminary
    pages use roman numbers
